# 02 · Typography

> **Status:** Stub. Structure defined; content pending a dedicated drafting cycle.

## Purpose

Governs the type system: families, scale, weights, and the rules for setting text
so it stays legible across web, terminal, and print.

## What this chapter will define

- **Families** — a monospace face (the CRT/terminal lineage) and likely a humanist
  companion for long-form prose. Fallback stacks per medium.
- **Scale** — a modular type scale (steps, ratio) shared with [03 · Space & Grid](03-space-grid.md).
- **Weights and styles** — the permitted set; what each is for.
- **Measure and leading** — line length and line-height rules.
- **Terminal constraints** — how type degrades to fixed-cell, single-weight grids.

## Open questions

- One family (mono everywhere, honoring the lineage) or two (mono + prose)?
- Do we ship/recommend specific open-license faces, or specify by metrics only?
