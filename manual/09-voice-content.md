# 09 · Voice & Content

> **Status:** Stub. Structure defined; content pending a dedicated drafting cycle.

## Purpose

Governs language: tone, terminology, and the rules for writing UI text, messages,
and documentation.

## What this chapter will define

- **Voice** — direct, precise, no filler. (Consistent with the house style: name
  the problem, skip the reassurance.)
- **Status language** — the exact words paired with each status step and OASIS
  value, so the prose matches the color.
- **Terminology** — a controlled vocabulary; one term per concept.
- **Message structure** — error, warning, and confirmation copy patterns.

## Open questions

- How tightly do we bind status wording to the OASIS enums' own labels vs. plainer
  synonyms for end users?

## Relation to other chapters

- Status wording must agree with [01 · Color](01-color.md) §3–§4.
- Message copy is consumed by [07 · Patterns](07-patterns.md).
